The problem

Every outage feels new when the fix lives in someone's head.

During an outage, the team searches chat, tickets, and old notes while customers wait. The person who solved the last one may be offline.

Incident Memory keeps the report, verified fix, and what failed together—then brings related resolutions back when a similar problem returns.

A useful loop

How it works

  1. 01

    Report from anywhere

    Capture the symptoms from the web, monitoring, support, or Band.

  2. 02

    Agent recalls similar incidents

    Bring back related fixes and the approaches that did not work.

  3. 03

    Team resolves it

    Use the briefing as context while people investigate and decide.

  4. 04

    The fix is remembered

    Save the verified resolution for the next report.
